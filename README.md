# Holding the Space: guided wilderness therapy walk

A self-led 20-minute guided nature walk from **Rewilding Therapies** and **The Outdoor Practice**.

- Audio walk guided by Luke Slater
- Safety framing, "Holding the space" agreements for walking with friends
- Reflection spaces, nature-as-mirror exercises, strengths and values prompts
- Download your reflections as PDF or text (saved on your own device only)
- Support tab with UK crisis lines and steadying tools

This is a wellbeing practice, not therapy or crisis support.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole page, self-contained
- `audio/holding-the-space-walk.m4a`: the walk recording
- `images/rewilding-logo.jpg`: player artwork
